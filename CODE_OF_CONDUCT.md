# 社区行为准则 / Code of Conduct

SchemCP 社区致力于提供专业、尊重、安全的协作环境。

We are committed to a professional, respectful, and safe collaboration environment.

参与者应：

- 以技术事实和可复现实证讨论问题；
- 尊重不同经验、背景和观点；
- 给出具体、可执行的评审意见；
- 保护用户工程、凭据和未公开漏洞；
- 在分歧中聚焦行为、代码和证据，而不是个人。

Participants should discuss reproducible evidence, respect different backgrounds and viewpoints, provide actionable reviews, protect private data and undisclosed vulnerabilities, and focus disagreements on behavior, code, and evidence rather than individuals.

不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开他人隐私、恶意破坏或持续的人身攻击。维护者可以编辑或删除不当内容，并对违规参与者进行警告、临时限制或永久移除。

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, sabotage, and persistent personal attacks are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants.

行为问题请私下报告给维护者 `yuanzihao`。维护者会尽量保护报告者身份，并避免利益冲突。

Report conduct concerns privately to `yuanzihao`. Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality where practical and avoid conflicts of interest.
